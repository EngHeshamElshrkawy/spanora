Spanora — Branding concepts

This round is based on the red bird reference. The neck and body form the “S” of Spanora, and the tail feathers fall like a flamenco skirt in Spanish red and gold. It has a bold ink outline, and a few gold brush strokes on the body. Icons are shown at 140 / 64 / 32 / 16 px. On dark backgrounds, a thin cream edge keeps the outline visible.

  • Bird red #D7263D
  • Spanish red #C1122F
  • Spanish gold #FFC72C
  • Peach #FFE3C4
  • Cream #FFF6E8
  • Ink #2B1B1F
